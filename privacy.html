Privacy Policy

This tool is a personal application for creating eBay listings from item photos. This policy explains what it does and does not do with your data.

What we collect

  • Photos you upload. They are resized in your browser and sent to our server only to generate listing text. They are not stored after the listing is written.
  • Your eBay connection. If you connect eBay, an encrypted token is stored in a cookie in your browser so the app can post listings on your behalf. We never see your eBay password.

What we share

  • Photos and listing text are sent to the AI provider that writes the listing and, when you choose to post, to eBay to create your listing.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else.

Your control

  • You can disconnect eBay at any time, which deletes the stored token.
  • You can clear your browser cookies to remove the stored connection.

Data retention

Listing text and uploaded photos are processed in memory to produce a listing and are not retained on our servers.
